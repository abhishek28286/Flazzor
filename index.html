About Flazzor Interiors

Creating beautiful, practical and functional spaces for modern homes.

About Our Company

Flazzor Interiors is a Udaipur-based interior and furniture solutions brand, focused on creating beautiful, practical and functional spaces for modern homes.

We specialize in Modular Kitchens, Wardrobes, Beds, TV Units, Dressing Tables, CNC Mandirs and other customized interior solutions.

Our approach is simple — understand the customer's requirements, make the best use of available space, and create designs that match their lifestyle, taste and budget.

From material selection and design to finishing and installation, we focus on quality workmanship, attention to detail and customer satisfaction.

About the Founder

The Vision Behind Flazzor Interiors

Flazzor Interiors was built with a simple vision — to make quality interior solutions accessible, practical and personalized for every home.

The founder Mr. Abhishek Agrawal believes that a good interior is not just about how beautiful it looks. It should also be strong, functional, space-efficient and comfortable for everyday use.

With a customer-first approach, every project is handled with attention to the client's requirements, preferences and budget.

The goal is to build long-term relationships with customers through quality work, honest communication and reliable service.From 2008 to 2026, our Founder has successfully completed 800+ residential, commercial, government, and private projects, building a strong reputation through quality, customization, and commitment. From Udaipur to cities such as Bhilwara, Jaipur, Indore, Agra, Jodhpur, Bhopal, and beyond, every project reflects years of experience, dedication, and client trust. Today, this journey stands as a proud story of 18+ years of excellence in interior design and furniture solutions.
