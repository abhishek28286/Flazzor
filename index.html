About Our Company

Flazzor Interiors is a Udaipur-based interior and furniture solutions brand, focused on creating beautiful, practical and functional spaces for modern homes.

We specialize in Modular Kitchens, Wardrobes, Beds, TV Units, Dressing Tables, CNC Mandirs and other customized interior solutions.

Our approach is simple — understand the customer's requirements, make the best use of available space, and create designs that match their lifestyle, taste and budget.

From material selection and design to finishing and installation, we focus on quality workmanship, attention to detail and customer satisfaction.

About the Founder

The Vision Behind Flazzor Interiors

Flazzor Interiors was built with a simple vision — to make quality interior solutions accessible, practical and personalized for every home.

The founder believes that a good interior is not just about how beautiful it looks. It should also be strong, functional, space-efficient and comfortable for everyday use.

With a customer-first approach, every project is handled with attention to the client's requirements, preferences and budget.

The goal is to build long-term relationships with customers through quality work, honest communication and reliable service.
